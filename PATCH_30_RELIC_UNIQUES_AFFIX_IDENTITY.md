# Patch 30 — Relic Uniques and Affix Identity

Adds the second itemization layer: build-defining uniques/relics, skill-tag affixes, forge-potential risk actions, appraisal, richer item cards, build-aware comparison, loot priority labels, specialized stash categories, and clearer forge previews.

Fresh saves receive starter currency/seals/runes and a couple of test relics. Existing saves receive the new seed once through `materials._item_identity_seeded_030`.
